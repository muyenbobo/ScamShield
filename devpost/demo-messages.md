# ScamShield Demo Messages

Every sample below is fictional and written for demonstration. The `.invalid` links are intentionally unusable. Paste them manually into ScamShield; they are not built-in app examples.

## Nigeria: fake bank security alert

> CEDAR UNION BANK SECURITY ALERT: Your account will be frozen tonight unless your BVN is revalidated within 2 hours. Confirm your BVN and PIN at https://cedarunion.example.invalid/verify.

Expected demonstration: High risk, with pressure to act quickly, impersonated bank authority, and a suspicious credential link.

## Nigeria: NIN renewal threat

> NATIONAL IDENTITY RENEWAL DESK: Your NIN will be deactivated today. Avoid a ₦25,000 penalty by confirming your NIN and date of birth now at https://nin-renewal.example.invalid/confirm.

Expected demonstration: High risk, with a threat and deadline, an official-sounding identity claim, and a request for sensitive information through an untrusted link.

## US/UK: unexpected tax refund

> HM Revenue & Customs refund notice: You are due a £742 tax refund. Claim it within 24 hours by entering your bank details at https://hmrc-refund.example.invalid/claim.

Expected demonstration: High risk, with an unexpected financial offer, urgency, authority impersonation, and a suspicious link.

## Legitimate control: routine meeting update

> Hi Ada, our project check-in is scheduled for 10:00 tomorrow in the main meeting room. Please bring your notes from last week. Thanks, Maya.

Expected demonstration: Low risk, with no common scam tactics detected. Low risk does not verify the sender or guarantee that a message is safe.

## Demo handling

- These examples contain no real personal, account, or financial details.
- Do not replace the fictional names or `.invalid` domains with real institutions, links, or someone's private message.
- Gemini free-tier processing is disclosed in the app; continue to avoid sensitive information during demos.
